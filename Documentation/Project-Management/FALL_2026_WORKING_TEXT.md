# Fall 2026 Working Text

## Editorial designation

The **Fall 2026 working text** for *Flashlights in the Dark* is the twin-poem
draft assembled in the August 17, 2026 working meeting. It is the text to use
for all Fall 2026 libretto discussion,
rehearsal preparation, score-engraving decisions, and singer/audience reading
materials until the collaborators deliberately approve a replacement.

This is an editorial designation. It does **not** by itself revise, engrave,
or replace any MusicXML, Finale, Pro Tools, event-recipe, conductor, or client
asset in this repository.

## Canonical captured snapshot

The exact, immutable 88-cue capture is maintained in the Communiti source

`Communiti/components/ToolsForArtists/flashlightsAssemblyTwinPoem.js`

The public reading presentation is:

`https://simphoni.ai/flashlights/twin-poem`

The capture uses the existing 88 stable cue IDs and their established
Shadow/Light assignments. Its seven chronological sections are **Opening**,
**Turn**, **Dawns**, **Darkness**, **Warmth**, **Grace**, and **Afterimage**.
Those IDs and cue onsets are the handoff contract between the text, a future
engraving pass, rehearsal materials, and the browser display.

## Editorial status and change control

- **Status:** working text for the Fall 2026 edition; not a final engraved
  score and not a claim that the legacy score assets already contain this text.
- **Live collaboration:** the `Assemble` tab at
  `https://simphoni.ai/flashlights/text` remains the shared editable meeting
  workspace. Its Firestore record is not a substitute for a documented
  editorial snapshot.
- **Published baseline:** the 88-cue file above is the current documented
  snapshot of the Assembly draft. The Twin Poem page reads that static capture
  so a later live edit cannot silently change the published reading copy.
- **When the team approves a revision:** update the Assembly record, capture a
  new complete 88-cue snapshot, update this document's date and decision note,
  then intentionally carry the text into notation/score assets. Do not edit
  generated event recipes or runtime copies to change libretto text.
- **When engraving begins:** preserve the cue IDs, Shadow/Light allocation,
  and timing map unless the collaborators also approve a musical/timing change.
  Any changed score must then regenerate downstream score-derived artifacts
  through the normal score and recipe workflow.
